> Review date: 2026-05-08. Manifesto: arnaudgelas/agentic-engineering-manifesto@08123c263794b5cc24dbb3e1a5897a74288026e9. Framework: OpenSpec 1.3.1. Organisation: Allianz. Industry: European insurance and financial services — SR 11-7, DORA, EU AI Act, GDPR, Solvency II.

## What OpenSpec asserts about this principle

OpenSpec asserts that proposal/design/tasks/spec artefacts together constitute an "audit trail" that "preserves the full context of every change — not just what changed, but the proposal explaining why, the design explaining how, and the tasks showing the work done" (`OpenSpec/docs/concepts.md` line 685, "Why Archive Matters"). The flow diagram (`OpenSpec/docs/concepts.md` lines 354–356) labels the four artefact types as `proposal → specs → design → tasks` with the captions `why / what / how / steps`. The `openspec-conventions` capability (`OpenSpec/openspec/specs/openspec-conventions/spec.md`) names a "Review" step where "Humans review proposal and future state" (line 466). No source file in `OpenSpec/openspec/specs/` names an accountable human, an approver role, an override rate, an irreversibility window, an oversight pattern, a sign-off authority, or a deployment-blocking gate; the `/opsx:verify` skill (`OpenSpec/openspec/specs/opsx-verify-skill/spec.md`) is explicitly non-blocking ("Does not block archive, but surfaces issues" — `OpenSpec/docs/commands.md` line 336).

## Structured Recovery Test

### Part A — Oversight Adequacy

OpenSpec instantiates **no** named oversight pattern from the four defined in `manifesto-principles.md` §5 (HITL synchronous, HITL asynchronous, HOTL, HOLL, EDL). The closest construct is the convention sentence "Humans review proposal and future state" (`OpenSpec/openspec/specs/openspec-conventions/spec.md` line 466). It is described once and is not bound to a measurable signal.

- **HITL.** *Absent.* No artefact in `OpenSpec/openspec/specs/` records reviewer identity, review timestamp, override rate, reviewer agreement rate, or review latency by work-item class. `OpenSpec/openspec/specs/cli-archive/spec.md` archives a change once `tasks.md` checkboxes are ticked; it does not record who approved, what they overrode, or how long they spent. The rubber-stamping detection signals from `adoption/metrics.md` ("Median review time per agent-generated PR", "PR rejection rate (agent-generated)", "Inline comments per approved PR", "Rework rate within 1 week of merge") have zero counterparts in `OpenSpec/openspec/specs/`.
- **HOTL.** *Absent.* The framework has no concept of an irreversibility window. `OpenSpec/openspec/specs/cli-archive/spec.md` performs an immediate folder move with delta-merge into `openspec/specs/`; no monitoring detection time, notification time, assessment time, or intervention time is defined. Per `manifesto-principles.md` §5 P5 minimum bar this means HOTL is "the appearance of oversight".
- **HOLL.** *Absent.* Per-action evidence is the markdown artefact set (`proposal.md`, `design.md`, `tasks.md`, delta `spec.md`); none of these include the foundation-model identifier, system-instruction hash, tool manifest, memory state version, retrieval corpus version, or composite state version that `governance/evidence-bundle-schema.md` (`aem_components`) requires for accountability reconstruction without a human witness. A new auditor cannot determine which model produced which proposal text from OpenSpec's archive alone.
- **EDL.** *Absent.* No artefact in `OpenSpec/openspec/specs/` defines an independent validator role, records validator domain qualifications, or stores a structured judgement-rationale record. The `/opsx:verify` skill (`OpenSpec/openspec/specs/opsx-verify-skill/spec.md`, "Verification Report Format") emits a CRITICAL/WARNING/SUGGESTION list executed by the implementing agent itself.

The waiver-expiry, feedback-loop closure, and claim-revalidation SLOs in `operational-templates/slo-table.md` have no counterpart in `OpenSpec/openspec/specs/`. The AEM column of `governance/authority-accountability-matrix.md` (rows: envelope decision, kill-switch, waiver, decommissioning, specification readiness, release) cannot be populated from OpenSpec artefacts because OpenSpec defines none of those authorities. Per `integration/low-consequence-resolution.md` AEM has no consequence-class carve-out — the per-action accountability minimum bar applies uniformly — so OpenSpec cannot escape the bar by classifying agent changes as "low consequence".

**Part A verdict: every oversight pattern is Absent.** OpenSpec operates structurally at Tier 2 or above for any production agent change (the agent edits production code through `/opsx:apply` and the change is archived without a blocking validator), so the binding constraint in §4.6 of the per-principle prompt fires.

### Part B — Structured Recovery

1. **Intent recovery.** *Pass.* `OpenSpec/openspec/specs/openspec-conventions/spec.md` mandates `proposal.md` capturing "**intent**, **scope**, and **approach**" (lines 364–368) with mandatory "## Intent", "## Scope" (in/out of scope), and "## Approach" sections. A new engineer reading an archived change folder can determine what the original engineer was trying to achieve from the proposal alone. The example template (`OpenSpec/docs/concepts.md` lines 365–388) is concrete.
2. **Decision recovery.** *Partial.* `design.md` is described as capturing "**technical approach** and **architecture decisions**" using `### Decision: <name>` headings with explicit rationale (`OpenSpec/docs/concepts.md` lines 400–420). However `OpenSpec/openspec/specs/openspec-conventions/spec.md` Requirement: "Progressive Rigor" makes design.md *optional* for "local and low-risk" changes ("authors use concise, behavior-first requirements with minimal ceremony"), and `OpenSpec/openspec/specs/opsx-verify-skill/spec.md` Requirement: "Coherence Verification" Scenario "No design.md" instructs the agent to "skip design adherence check". Decisions are recoverable when design.md exists; for changes archived without one, decision rationale is unrecoverable.
3. **Evidence recovery.** *Partial.* The change folder structure (`OpenSpec/openspec/specs/openspec-conventions/spec.md` "Project Structure", `OpenSpec/docs/concepts.md` lines 320–327) co-locates `proposal.md`, `design.md`, `tasks.md`, and delta `specs/` in one directory and the archive preserves them with a date prefix (`changes/archive/YYYY-MM-DD-[name]/`). However the artefacts that `manifesto-done.md` calls evidence — evaluation reports with pass/fail and metrics, trace IDs, deployment IDs, rollback plans, policy check outputs, memory updates, security static analysis results — have no slot in the OpenSpec archive structure. The agentic provenance record fields formalised in `operational-templates/agentic-provenance-record.json` (foundation model identifier, system-instruction hash, tool manifest, retrieval corpus version) have no field in any OpenSpec artefact.
4. **Reproduction.** *Fail.* No file in `OpenSpec/openspec/specs/` records the foundation-model identifier, model version, provider category, system-instruction hash, tool manifest, memory state version, retrieval corpus version, embedding model version, or dataset lineage that produced any artefact. `governance/evidence-bundle-schema.md` `aem_components` and `operational-templates/agentic-provenance-record.json` formalise these fields — OpenSpec stores none. A new engineer given the same prompt and the same OpenSpec change cannot reproduce the same output because the relevant configuration is not captured. Bundle integrity attestation (cryptographic hash or digital signature per `manifesto-done.md` Hardening DoD) is also absent.
5. **Modification.** *Partial.* The `### Requirement:` / `#### Scenario:` Given/When/Then structure (`OpenSpec/openspec/specs/openspec-conventions/spec.md` Requirement: "Structured Format for Behavioral Specs") gives a downstream engineer named requirements to preserve, and `/opsx:verify` (`OpenSpec/openspec/specs/opsx-verify-skill/spec.md` Requirement: "Coherence Verification") flags divergence as WARNING. However scenarios are prose, not executable evaluations — a modifier cannot verify that an undocumented invariant has been preserved because no executable invariant test exists, and the verify skill is non-blocking ("Does not block archive" — `OpenSpec/docs/commands.md` line 336). Modification is safe at the structural layer and unsafe at the semantic layer.

**Part B count.** Fully passing: **1** (Intent). Partial: 3 (Decision, Evidence, Modification). Failing: 1 (Reproduction). Per the binding-constraint table in §4.6 of the per-principle prompt, "0–1 steps fully passing → 0–19". Partial passes provide credit within the band.

**Part A binding constraint.** Part A finds every oversight pattern Absent for action classes OpenSpec operates at Tier 2 or above. Per §4.6 the score cannot exceed the upper bound of the band one step lower than Part B alone would set. Part B is already in the lowest band (0–19), so the binding constraint does not pull the score further down but reinforces that the upper region of the 0–19 band is unreachable. Final score lies in the upper-middle of the 0–19 band, reflecting credit for three partial recovery steps.

## What works

- `OpenSpec/openspec/specs/openspec-conventions/spec.md` Requirement: "Project Structure" mandates that every change folder contain `proposal.md` (intent/scope/approach), optional `design.md`, `tasks.md`, and delta `specs/` co-located in one directory, giving a single locatable artefact set per change for any future investigator.
- `OpenSpec/docs/concepts.md` "Why Archive Matters" (line 685) preserves the full change folder under `changes/archive/YYYY-MM-DD-[name]/` so that a new engineer can locate intent, design rationale, and tasks for a historical change without git archaeology.
- `OpenSpec/openspec/specs/openspec-conventions/spec.md` Requirement: "Structured Format for Behavioral Specs" enforces `### Requirement: [Name]` and `#### Scenario: [Description]` headings with RFC 2119 keywords, producing parseable, named units of behaviour that a new engineer can map back to original intent.
- `OpenSpec/openspec/specs/opsx-verify-skill/spec.md` Requirement: "Verification Report Format" prescribes a CRITICAL/WARNING/SUGGESTION report with "specific, actionable fix recommendation" and bans vague language, providing one small empirical anchor against rubber-stamping at the review-output layer.
- `OpenSpec/openspec/specs/cli-validate/spec.md` Requirement: "JSON output schema for bulk validation" emits a stable `{ items[], summary, version }` JSON shape, which an external accountability layer outside OpenSpec can consume to enforce a blocking gate the framework itself does not provide.
- `OpenSpec/docs/concepts.md` Decision template (`### Decision: <name>` with explicit rationale, lines 400–420) gives a recognised authoring pattern for capturing why architectural choices were made, when authors use it.

## Where it fails the manifesto's bar

- "If no named human can inspect the reasoning, review the evidence, and own the outcome of a production agent, the system is ungoverned" (`manifesto-principles.md` P12 minimum bar). No artefact in `OpenSpec/openspec/specs/` names an accountable human, records reviewer identity, or binds an outcome to a named owner; `OpenSpec/openspec/specs/cli-archive/spec.md` archives on tasks-checked alone. This is absent — not partial — and exposes Allianz to EIOPA AI Guidelines 2021 "board-level accountability for AI systems" (`domains/insurance.md` line 109) and Solvency II Art. 41 (system of governance) failures.
- "If you cannot report, for each oversight pattern in use, the metrics that indicate whether the pattern is delivering genuine governance — override rates for HITL, false negative rates for HOTL, compliance audit currency for HOLL, expert qualification currency for EDL — you cannot claim the governance is functioning. You can only claim it is present" (`manifesto-principles.md` P12 minimum bar — oversight adequacy). OpenSpec records none of these metrics in any artefact. This is convention-not-enforcement and breaches DORA Art. 5 (ICT governance and control framework) which requires the management body to assess effectiveness of internal-governance arrangements.
- "Agents may not accept residual risk, approve production exposure, waive controls, or absorb accountability for business outcomes" (`manifesto-principles.md` P12). `/opsx:verify` (`OpenSpec/openspec/specs/opsx-verify-skill/spec.md`) is run by the same agent that produced the implementation, and `OpenSpec/docs/commands.md` line 336 confirms it "Does not block archive". The agent effectively self-clears its own work. This is absent oversight separation and breaches SR 11-7 §V (effective challenge) and Solvency II Art. 48 (actuarial function independence).
- "Accountability without visibility is a legal fiction. You cannot own what you cannot see" (`manifesto-principles.md` P12). OpenSpec's archive contains no foundation-model identifier, system-instruction hash, tool manifest, retrieval corpus version, or composite state version — the visibility fields formalised in `governance/evidence-bundle-schema.md` `aem_components` and `operational-templates/agentic-provenance-record.json`. This is absent provenance and breaches EU AI Act Art. 12 (record-keeping) and Art. 13 (transparency and provision of information to deployers) for high-risk AI systems.
- "When trace volume exceeds meaningful review capacity, the correct response is to raise automation barriers (tighten evaluation thresholds, lower autonomy tiers) until oversight signal quality is restored — not to accept degraded oversight as a workload problem" (`manifesto-principles.md` P12). OpenSpec defines no autonomy-tier construct, no automation-barrier mechanism, and no signal-quality measurement. This is scope-gap-with-flag at the framework level and breaches EU AI Act Art. 14 (human oversight) which requires deployers to assign oversight to natural persons with the necessary competence and authority.
- "Accountability diffusion — the accountable human's name is on the record, but their actual review was nominal... Detected by: override rate at or near zero for work item classes with known complexity; reviewer agreement rate above 95% for sustained periods; review latency consistently below the minimum plausible for the case type" (`manifesto-principles.md` P12). `OpenSpec/openspec/specs/cli-archive/spec.md` records none of override rate, reviewer agreement rate, or review latency. This is absent rubber-stamping detection and breaches DORA Art. 6(2) (ICT risk-management framework) and the EIOPA AI Governance expectation cited in `domains/insurance.md` line 109.
- "Accountability abstraction — the oversight design nominally provides HOLL, but the per-action evidence record is insufficient to reconstruct what happened and why" (`manifesto-principles.md` P12). The OpenSpec archive lacks per-action provenance fields; an auditor cannot, for a specific archived change, confirm which model, prompt, and tool manifest produced the artefacts. This breaches Solvency II Art. 121 (statistical quality standards — internal model documentation) for any agent-assisted internal-model artefact.
- "The accountable human reviews the evidence bundle, accepts that the DoD conditions are satisfied, and accepts production accountability for the outcome (P12). This is not a rubber stamp — it is the governance record that the evidence was reviewed" (`manifesto.md` quoted in `integration/low-consequence-resolution.md` §1.1). OpenSpec has no evidence-bundle construct, no DoD-condition record, and no governance record of acceptance. This is absent and breaches GDPR Art. 22 (automated individual decision-making) safeguards which require demonstrable human intervention on consequential decisions.

## Allianz-specific implications

- Solvency II Art. 41 (system of governance), Art. 44 (risk management), and Art. 48 (actuarial function) require named, organisationally separate accountability with documented reviewer qualifications. OpenSpec records no reviewer identity in `OpenSpec/openspec/specs/cli-archive/spec.md` or any other artefact. Allianz MUST add a mandatory accountable-human field to `proposal.md`, a separate validator-sign-off field gated by an organisationally independent role, and refuse `openspec archive` if either is missing for any change touching a Solvency II internal-model agent.
- EIOPA AI and ML Guidelines 2021 (cited in `domains/insurance.md` lines 102–115) require board-level accountability for AI systems and integration of AI governance into the system of governance. OpenSpec exposes no board-level attestation slot. Allianz MUST extend the OpenSpec change-creation flow (`OpenSpec/openspec/specs/change-creation/spec.md`) with a board-attestation artefact for high-impact insurance agent changes and bind archive to its presence and currency.
- DORA Art. 5 (ICT governance), Art. 6 (ICT risk-management framework), and Art. 28–30 (ICT third-party risk management) require management-body assessment of governance effectiveness with documented metrics. OpenSpec records no override rate, reviewer agreement rate, or review latency. Allianz MUST instrument the rubber-stamping signals in `adoption/metrics.md` ("Median review time per agent-generated PR", "PR rejection rate (agent-generated)", "Inline comments per approved PR", "Rework rate within 1 week of merge") in the code-review platform that wraps OpenSpec, and report them to the management body quarterly.
- EU AI Act Art. 12 (record-keeping), Art. 13 (transparency), and Art. 14 (human oversight) require automatic logs of operation and oversight by competent natural persons for high-risk AI systems. OpenSpec captures no foundation-model identifier, system-instruction hash, tool manifest, or oversight-actor record. Allianz MUST attach an agentic-provenance record (per `operational-templates/agentic-provenance-record.json`) to every archived change for any agent operating in EU AI Act high-risk scope (claims triage, life and health underwriting, fraud scoring) and refuse archive when any provenance field is missing.
- SR 11-7 §V (effective challenge) and §IV.A (model documentation) require challenge by parties independent of model development with sign-off authority. The non-blocking `/opsx:verify` skill (`OpenSpec/docs/commands.md` line 336: "Does not block archive") run by the implementing agent fails both. Allianz MUST insert a second-line model-risk-management gate that consumes the JSON output from `OpenSpec/openspec/specs/cli-validate/spec.md` ("JSON output schema for bulk validation") and is the sole authority over deployment for any agent change in SR 11-7 scope; OpenSpec archive completion does not authorise production deployment.
